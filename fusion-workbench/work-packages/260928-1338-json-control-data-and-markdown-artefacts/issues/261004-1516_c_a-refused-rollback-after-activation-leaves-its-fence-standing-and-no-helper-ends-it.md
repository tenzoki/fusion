A refused rollback after activation leaves its fence standing, and no helper ends it
---
`bin/fusion-migrate rollback` on an activated store first sends `maintenance begin` and only then the first rollback chunk (`hooks/migrate.ts`, `rollback`). When that chunk is refused, for example `conflict/after-state-changed` because ordinary work landed after activation, the helper exits 8 and leaves the fence standing. Every later mutation is then refused as `maintenance-active`. The helper names no way out. `rollback` sends the same refused chunk again. `resume` answers "is being or was rolled back; `rollback` finishes that". `bin/fusion-write`'s hint attributes the fence to an archive move and points at `bin/fusion-archive`. `skills/migrate/SKILL.md` Step 7 covers exit 8 only with "show the reason and stop". A codec `maintenance end` naming the fence, sent by hand through `bin/fusion-record`, ends it.

**Cross-references:** 261004-1516-fj04-step12-rerun-proof-on-fresh-copies.md, 261001-1804_*_plan-fj04-the-migration-of-a-legacy-workbench-proven-on-copies.md

**Evidence.** Step 12's re-run (the analysis above, finding F1), at `0b1e1b58` installed as 13.0.0, on all three copies: one `bin/fusion-write create` after activation, then `rollback`, exit 8, and `status` shows `fence=<id>` with `state=json-control`. On a clone of fusion's copy, `fusion-write create` was then refused with `conflict/maintenance-active`, a second `rollback` exited 8 again, and `resume` exited 5. A `{"op":"maintenance","action":"end","fence":<id>}` request through the installed `bin/fusion-record` answered `ok`, and `status` then showed `fence=-`. The codec's recorded session base D ends such a refusal with an explicit `end` (`codec/README.md`, the `protocol-session-migration` row), so the codec side is as specified. The gap is the host helper and the skill.

**Acceptance.** After a post-activation rollback that the codec refuses, the store either carries no fence, or the helper's stderr and `/fusion:migrate` Step 7 name the one command that ends the fence. Either way, a `create` afterwards lands. `bin/fusion-write`'s fence hint does not name an archive move when the fence is a rollback's. A case in `hooks/lib/__tests__/migrate.test.ts` shows: create, then rollback refused, then the named command, then create lands.

**Resolved (2026-10-04).** First acceptance arm: when the codec refuses the first rollback chunk while the store is still activated, `rollback()` in `hooks/migrate.ts` sends `maintenance end` for the fence it began (`endRefusedFence`, under the stored `end_id`, clearing `s.rollback` only once the answer is stored) and exits 8 naming the refusal and that the fence ended; `status` shows `fence=-` and a `create` lands. Cost, stated on stderr and in `bin/fusion-migrate`'s header: the ended begin/end pair is a later operation to the codec, so every later rollback of that migration is refused too; the only case newly lost is a hand-reverted change with no codec operation. `bin/fusion-write`'s fence hint (`hooks/write.ts`) is neutral, naming the archive and the migration ways out, since the fence file names no owner. `migrate.test.ts` covers create, refused rollback, `fence=-`, refused second rollback, create lands. `codec/` untouched. Resolved by the commit that renames this record to `_c_`.
