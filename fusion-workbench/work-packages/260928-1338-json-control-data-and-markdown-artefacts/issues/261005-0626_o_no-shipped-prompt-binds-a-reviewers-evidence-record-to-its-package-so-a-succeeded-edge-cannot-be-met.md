No shipped prompt binds a reviewer's evidence record to its package, so a succeeded edge cannot be met
---
FJ03d step 6 gave the reviewer `bin/fusion-write evidence`, which files an evidence record beside its review. Nothing in the shipped text sends `attach-evidence`, the subcommand that binds that record to the package. Until somebody does, no package carries an accepted evidence binding, and a `depends_on` entry with condition `succeeded` stays unmet whatever the review found.

Resolved: decision `261007-1836-which-party-binds-a-closing-reviews-evidence-into-the-finish-so-a-succeeded-edge-can-be-met.md`, answered by the user with option 1, carried out by steps 3 to 5 of plan `261007-1836-plan-the-four-open-defects-fixed-before-v13-is-tested.md` in `89471d97`. The issue's acceptance asked for a party that sends `attach-evidence`; the decision found that question cut one step too early, because `succeeded` reads `outcome.evidence` alone and `attach-evidence` writes the package's top-level `evidence` on a live package, which nothing copies into the outcome. So no prompt sends `attach-evidence`: the finish binds the evidence itself. `bin/fusion-write transition --to done --outcome … --evidence <evidence control path>` composes each entry as `attach-evidence` does (step 3), and `agents/orchestrator.md` `## Closing a work package` step 4 sends the **Finish** with the closing review's `.evidence.json` before it appends the closure note, since the evidence binds the brief's bytes (step 5). Proof through the shipped CLI, `hooks/lib/__tests__/record-write.test.ts` `describe("a succeeded edge, through bin/fusion-write and bin/fusion-work-order")` (step 4): "B is blocked while A is live, and ready with no unmet row once A is finished with --evidence naming its accepted review, the closure note appended after it included", with the controls "a finish without --evidence leaves B blocked on an unmet row naming the missing evidence", "a revise verdict bound the same way lands and leaves B blocked on an unmet row" and "a closure note appended to A's brief before the finish makes the finish refused brief-changed, exit 6, and A stays claimed"; the client flag in `describe("transition --evidence")`. Proof through the shipped prompt, step 8's case (e) in `261007-2348-agent-dispatch-and-skill-block-observation-at-495aca7d.md`: a headless orchestrator closed A `done` with one `outcome.evidence` entry bound through the finish (top-level `evidence` empty, verdict `accept`, the note written after the status), and `bin/fusion-work-order` reported B `ready` with no `unmet=` row.
