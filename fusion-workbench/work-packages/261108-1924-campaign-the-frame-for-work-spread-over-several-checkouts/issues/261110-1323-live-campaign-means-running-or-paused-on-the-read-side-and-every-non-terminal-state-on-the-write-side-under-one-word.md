"Live campaign" means running or paused on the read side and every non-terminal state on the write side, under one word
---
`hooks/lib/campaign.ts:541` `LIVE_STATES` is `running` and `paused`: the overview evaluates lanes and conditions over those, `hooks/lib/campaign-sync.ts:126` `mineOf` picks `mine` among those, and `docs/campaign.md` says "The lane view is evaluated for a running or paused campaign only". `hooks/lib/campaign-write.ts:269` `campaignOf` reads "live" as every `state.state` outside `TERMINAL_STATES` (`completed`, `bounded`, `failed`, `cancelled`, `archived`, `discarded`), so `draft` and `authorised`, the states every campaign Prior writes starts in, are live for the writes and not for the reads. `bin/fusion-campaign` uses the one word for both sets (`lane=` row, "each LIVE readable campaign (`running` or `paused`)", against `## The steering writes`, "the one live campaign of the workbench (a `state.state` outside …)"), and `skills/campaign/SKILL.md` Step 6 says `--campaign <id>` is needed "only where more than one campaign is live" without saying which reading. Two effects: a `draft` campaign beside a `running` one makes `pause`, `resume`, `move`, `reassign` and `end` without `--campaign` refuse `unknown-scope/several-campaigns` while `overview` and `sync` count one live campaign; and `lanes`, `move` and `reassign` accept a `draft` or `authorised` campaign whose lanes `sync` never reads as `mine` and the overview never evaluates, without saying so.
