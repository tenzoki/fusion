## Question

The Circle's Directive names four outcomes. The fourth is that
`shared/decisions/260816-0740_*_does-the-prose-register-get-a-measurable-gate-and-which-surface-does-it-measure.md`
comes to carry a measured number where it has carried an untested inference since 2026-08-16.

The measurement compares a pre-repair window of session prose against a post-repair window. Its own
second strengthening excludes this Circle's history files from the post-repair window, because a
session whose subject is em-dashes has a primed writer, and that exclusion is correct.

The consequence was not drawn in the spec. Once the repair lands, the only sessions producing history
files in this repository are this Circle's own. Verified independently: of the thirteen Circle records
on disk, one is active, one bounded, ten closed and one superseded, so no other Circle is live to
produce a session. The post-repair window therefore has **no members** before this Circle ends, and the
fourth outcome is not deliverable inside it.

## Options

1. **Bounded Closure.** Register the protocol, land the repair, and close the Circle with the
   measurement explicitly deferred to a later Circle. The record `_t_` moves to `_b_`.
2. **Narrow the exclusion** to "history files written by an agent that read this Circle's planning
   documents". Decidable, since a dispatch's inputs are recorded. Readmits sessions that ran in this
   Circle on unrelated work, of which there are none, so it buys nothing today.
3. **Drop the exclusion.** Not considered further. It removes the one confound the design can actually
   remove, in order to report a number that would then mean less.

## Answer: option 1, Bounded Closure.

Three of the four outcomes are deliverable in this Circle and will be delivered: the stale-copy refresh,
the corpus at its ceiling under a metric that does not count a file's own anti-examples, and the
fact-first requirement gaining the test that makes it applicable. The fourth is not, and no
rearrangement inside this Circle makes it so.

What Bounded Closure means here is precise rather than apologetic. The Artifact includes the registered
protocol, the pre-repair window captured before the repair landed, and the exact threshold. That is the
part of the fourth outcome that had to happen **before** the repair, and it is the part a later Circle
cannot reconstruct once the repair is in history. Deferring the measurement costs a later Circle one
analyst pass. Forcing it now would produce a number measured against an empty window, which is the
fabricated measurement this project has already filed a defect about once.

## What this forecloses

The Circle closes `_b_` and not `_c_`, and the user asked for a successful close. This record is the
honest reading of that instruction rather than a departure from it: a Circle reported coherent while one
of its four stated outcomes was silently dropped would be the failure the three-edge verdict exists to
catch.

The decision record `260816-0740` stays answered, and gains the protocol and the pre-repair number.
