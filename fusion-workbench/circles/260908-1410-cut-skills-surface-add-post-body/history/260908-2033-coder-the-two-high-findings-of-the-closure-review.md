## What changed

**Finding 1, the deleted state file.** The fix went on the cleanup side, following the `$DOMAIN` precedent
that already stands in the same step rather than moving Step 1's delete. `skills/cleanup/SKILL.md` Step 1
item 1 captures `session.git_head_at_start` and `session.history_file` beside the domain, before item 4
removes the file, with the `sed` one-liner `agents/orchestrator.md` already documents for the same read. An
unread field is held and handed on as `UNREAD`, so the message half is told the anchor could not be read and
is never given an empty string it cannot tell from a hash.

`skills/post/SKILL.md` `## Step 2: compose the draft` states the contract once: the two elements come from
the state file, a caller that deletes it hands them in instead, and an element whose value is unread is left
out of the pointer block and named in the report. No empty range, no bare `to HEAD`, no invented anchor. The
standalone shape resolves to that same omission rather than to a third compose-nothing condition, and
`## Step 3: nothing to say is an answer` says so: with no range to read, its second condition reads on the
records alone. `bin/fusion-events turns` reads the same field out of the same file and is unaffected, because
nothing moves or empties the file; it is read earlier.

**Finding 2, the question that may not be asked.** The coupling is stated once, in
`skills/cleanup/SKILL.md` `### The message half`, and it adds no place where the pipeline waits. The draft
rides the **first** `AskUserQuestion` call the `CLAUDE.md` half puts, which is ordinarily the gate and, where
the blast-radius stop fired, the scale confirmation, the call the user actually reaches. Where that half puts
no call at all, this half puts one, on the standalone shape `skills/post/SKILL.md` already defines.

**The reading of "one stop" this relies on** is the one the user ruled in
`260907-1942_*_does-the-pipelines-one-stop-permit-a-second-question-in-the-same-askuserquestion-call.md`: one
stop is one place where the pipeline waits, not one question. On the three rows the record lists as undecided
the count of such places goes from zero to one, never from one to two, so
`260827-1311_*_where-in-the-cleanup-pipeline-does-the-one-gate-stand.md` is not reopened and no ruling had to
be put back to the user. The text also says that a halt of the `CLAUDE.md` half is not a halt of Step 6, since
an executor reading the curate body's "stop" inline could otherwise take it for one.

`skills/curate/SKILL.md` was **not** edited. Its branches are correct as written; a second statement of the
coupling inside it is the drift shape this Circle has filed twice already. `skills/post/SKILL.md` `## Step 4`
now splits on whether the caller puts a call of its own rather than on which command was typed, which is what
makes the two bodies agree without either restating the other.
