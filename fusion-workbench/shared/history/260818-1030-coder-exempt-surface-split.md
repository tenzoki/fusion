## What changed

bullets. A **universal** group carries the two exemptions that survived on their own merits
— code and code comments, and hook and CLI operator strings — and keeps the
`hooks/session-start.ts` `## Why the message is English` citation as the worked case for the

own `rules/` and its own `README.md` included — which is precisely what the old text denied
it. A closing paragraph names this repository's double role and shows the criterion dividing
it rather than exempting it whole.

it pointed at "the exempt surfaces listed below" as one list and now names the two groups.
