### 1. `hooks/lib/staging-drift.ts` — `stagingSentence()`, the prohibition clause

The sentence forbids four shapes and claims each way of loosening the shape fails on its own.
Three of the four names reached a clause. "A glob" was unqualified in the prohibition half and
qualified as "unquoted" in the justification half, so a quoted git pathspec glob was forbidden by
the list and named by no clause — while the failure the sentence sat next to, under-staging, is
one the reviewer measured it not to exhibit. It stages the rename whole, exactly as the directory
argument does.

The over-staging clause now names three shapes instead of two, and the reverse clause says
"unquoted shell glob":

- **`-A`, a directory argument and a quoted pathspec glob** are the over-staging that shape
  prevents.
- **`-u`** keeps the deletion-without-successor mechanism, which the measurement shows it is the
  only shape to exhibit.
- **An unquoted shell glob** does the reverse — successor staged, deletion left behind, both names
  in HEAD.

All four forbidden names now reach a clause, which is the acceptance. The record's recommended
wording was taken as given; it costs six words.

**This supersedes the judgement recorded one task earlier.** `260817-2130`'s resolution note and
`history/260817-2138-coder-staging-sentence-per-shape-justification.md` both state a deliberate
decision *not* to name the quoted form separately, on the ground that the over-staging routing
already covered it. The reviewer's counter is that the routing named a **closed pair** — "`-A` and
a directory argument" — which a quoted glob is not a member of, so the coverage was asserted and
not written. Neither of those two earlier files was edited: they are the record of what was decided
then, and the disagreement is carried by `260817-2147`'s cross-reference to them, which is where a
reader meets it.
