## What was implemented

Step 1, the tail class. `SENTENCE_STOP`, the lookbehind `(?<![A-Za-z0-9_…*-]\.)`, is now the last
fragment of both `BARE_RE` and `REC_RE` in `hooks/lib/citation-scan.ts`. The tail stays greedy, takes
a sentence's full stop, fails the lookbehind and backtracks one character. `REC_RE` is in scope even
at `store-prefixed=0` because `readsBackWhole()` in `citation-sweep.ts` requires the rewritten string
to re-tokenise whole: fixing one tail alone would have made the sweep decline a rewrite it performs
today.

Step 2, the sweep directory. `circleDirs()` now indexes an archive sweep's own directory beside the
Circles inside it, reporting `archive/<sweep>` as the resolved path. The push-or-set arm was
extracted into a local `one()` so the sweep entry and the Circle entries share it and a repeated name
accumulates paths rather than overwriting.

Step 3, the head-field exemption, was **not** implemented. Its property is undecided and the clause
at `hooks/lib/citation-scan.ts` stands exactly as it was.
