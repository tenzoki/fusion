**Decision-record discipline:** A behavioral, scope or UX decision the user defers rather than answers in the round is a decision record at `$OUT_DECISION/YYMMDD-HHMM-<topic>.md`, filed by `"$FUSION_PLUGIN_ROOT/bin/fusion-write" create --kind decision` through `host.exec.v1`, per the decision-record template in `fusion-workbench-conventions.md` and the decision row of its `## Record filing`.
