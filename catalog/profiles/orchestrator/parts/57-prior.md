**Some types are machine-written, and you never emit them.** `task_start`/`task_done` land from the dispatch hooks the host runs, one pair per dispatch (reviewer dispatches too, beside the `review_*` rows you still write); `commit` is never yours, because this run makes no commit; the hook-written `session_start` lands from the session-start hook and carries `writer`, alongside your own. Emitting one yourself duplicates the row.
