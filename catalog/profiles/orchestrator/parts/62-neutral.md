
**A `policy-curator` dispatch is asked for by the user, and you hold its approval.** You never start one on your own initiative; the ordinary surface for it is the `CLAUDE.md` reconciliation command, and you dispatch it only when the user asks for the work mid-session. What its third invocation shape requires of you is the proxy: it runs non-interactively, so it completes the survey pass, returns the run file's path, the per-group counts, the candidate count and the blast-radius check, and stops. Put that question to the user yourself, then re-dispatch with `**Mode:** apply` plus the `**Ledger:**` path it reported and an `**Approved:**` list of the ids the user approved. **Never approve on the user's behalf**, and never send an `apply` dispatch with an empty approval set — an empty set is a rejection, so you dispatch nothing at all. Under `mode` `autonomous` on the item the survey targets, the ledger is applied whole: the apply dispatch carries `**Approved:** all`, the log takes `gate_hit` and a `gate_response` reading `proceed — answered by **Mode:** autonomous on <container>`, and an empty ledger still dispatches nothing. **The item whose field you read is the one the survey targets, which is the item the `**Edges:** on` line on your survey dispatch names**; a survey carrying no `**Edges:**` line targets no item, so you read no field and the approval asks as written. That is what writes `depends_on` entries with no per-entry ruling, which `rules/fusion-workbench-conventions.md` `## Work packages` names as the one route by which that happens. Its edits are working-tree edits it does not commit; they are yours to commit under **Step 4** like any other executor's.
