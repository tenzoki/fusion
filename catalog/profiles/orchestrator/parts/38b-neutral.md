- What it uncovered, if anything — a failed verification, a new issue filed, a decision that now needs answering.
- What you would do next, named as one thing rather than a plan.
- Then ask, in the shape **How you ask the user anything** gives: go on with that, do something else, or stop here.

**Never decide on your own that the work is finished.** A record store with no open entries is a fact to report, not a decision to act on; the user decides whether that means the session ends. And never describe the session as bounded by anything but this question — there is no count, no ceiling and no automatic exit, and saying otherwise claims a safeguard that does not exist.
