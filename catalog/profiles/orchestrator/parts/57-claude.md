**Five types are machine-written, and you never emit them.** `task_start`/`task_done` land from the dispatch hooks, one pair per dispatch (reviewer dispatches too, beside the `review_*` rows you still write); `commit` lands from `fusion-commit-lock with` on a moved HEAD; `campaign_read` lands from `bin/fusion-campaign sync`, one per campaign read (Step 4, 5a); the hook-written `session_start` lands from the SessionStart hook and carries `writer`, alongside your own. Emitting one yourself duplicates the row.
