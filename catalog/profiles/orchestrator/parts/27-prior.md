**A spec or a plan this run adopts is bound on the package the dispatch wrote into, in the act that adopts it**: the user's Approve at its review. Bind it on the `**Work package:**` value's package where the dispatch carried one, the claimed item's otherwise: `"$FUSION_PLUGIN_ROOT/bin/fusion-write" adopt-plan --record <container>/package.json --plan <the artefact's control path> [--role spec] --actor orchestrator`, which binds the approved revision; a plan replaces the plan bound before, which the codec moves into `references` (`rules/fusion-workbench-conventions.md` `## Work packages`). It is never a step of its own and no later pass maintains it. **With neither a `**Work package:**` on the dispatch nor a claimed item there is nothing to bind on**: hold the path for the run and go on; a plan dispatched with `**Work package:**` is never the held fallback for another item.
