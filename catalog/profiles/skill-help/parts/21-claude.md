**Coming from 13.5.1:** 13.6.0 lets campaign peers follow the steering session. A lane peer takes its lane's next package, and files every approval it would put to its own user as a question to the steering session; `/fusion:campaign` shows those questions and files the user's answer. The answer and every steering act are committed and pushed in the turn they are confirmed. Both sides wait on `bin/fusion-campaign watch --report`, a Bash background command that wakes only for a row to act on, reports an outage once and bounds each call to the remote at 60 s. Nothing migrates. Run `fusion --update` on every checkout, restart, and let Bash start the watch without a prompt. Detail: `$FUSION_SRC/docs/campaign.md` and `$FUSION_SRC/docs/upgrading-to-v13.md`.

Detail: `$FUSION_SRC/docs/campaign.md` and `$FUSION_SRC/docs/upgrading-to-v13.md`.
